## What this is
The marketing website for **Design Tribe** — a full-service design studio positioned as
**"Guided by humans. Powered by AI."** It sells design services (branding, rebranding, UX/UI,
website design + development, AI product design, design systems, content/marketing, presentations)
to ambitious organizations.

## Positioning
**Design Tribe is a _strategist_ — not a commodity design vendor.** In the AI era, we combine the
**speed of AI** with the **precision and strategic empathy of a human**. AI accelerates execution;
humans bring strategy, judgment, taste, and empathy. ("Guided by humans. Powered by AI." is the short
form.) Lead with strategic thinking — the studio is a thinking partner first, an execution shop second.
- Art direction that expresses this: **animation-driven, maximalist, creative** → `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`.

## Status
We are **rebuilding the site from scratch** with a new design language (→ `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`).
The old `codex/designtribe-static-home` build's **design is discarded** — we salvaged **only its
copy** (now in `CONTENT.md`). Do **not** use the old layout, components, or styling as a reference.

## Audience
- **Today:** primarily **corporate / enterprise** clients (the bulk of current business).
- **Shifting toward:** the **younger generation moving into decision-making power** — the buyers of
  the next several years.
- **Implication:** design for where the audience is *heading*, not only where it is — skew bolder,
  more creative, more motion-led to resonate with emerging decision-makers, **without alienating** the
  current corporate base.
- 🔲 How far to lean future vs. current corporate comfort? (calibrate the maximalism — see Design direction)
